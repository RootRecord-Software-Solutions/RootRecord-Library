## What was tested
If FLM is down and single-flight is IDLE, `run-infer.sh` runs `setsid nice -n 10 flm serve $FLM_MODEL --pmode balanced --ctx-len ${FLM_CTX_LEN:-4096} --port 52625`. It waits up to 45 s for `/v1/models`, runs the gated chat, and an EXIT trap stops the server (TERM, then KILL after 10 s). `FLM_ON_DEMAND=0` disables this. Warmup stays opt-in (`FLM_WARMUP_RESIDENT=1`).
